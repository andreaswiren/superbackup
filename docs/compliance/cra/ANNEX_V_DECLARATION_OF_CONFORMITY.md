# EU Declaration of Conformity — TEMPLATE, NOT ISSUED

> **This is a template. It is not a declaration of conformity, it has not been
> issued, it is not signed, and nothing in this repository may be treated as
> one.**
>
> No EU declaration of conformity exists for superbackup, and none may be
> issued by this project. superbackup is not made available on the market within
> the meaning of Article 3(22) — the reasoning is in [`README.md`](README.md) —
> so there is no manufacturer, no conformity assessment has been carried out
> under Article 32, and **no CE marking is or may be affixed**.
>
> The placeholders below are `[IN SQUARE BRACKETS]`. Every one of them requires
> a fact that only a real legal manufacturer possesses. This document
> deliberately invents none of them: no company, no address, no notified body,
> no certificate number, no signature. A declaration of conformity is a legal
> instrument by which a named person accepts liability, and a plausible-looking
> filled-in example is a forgery waiting to be copied.

This template exists so that a downstream commercial distributor — see
[`README.md`](README.md) §7 — has a correctly structured starting point, and so
that a reader can see exactly which facts would have to become true before
superbackup could be CE-marked.

## Structure required by Annex V

Article 28 requires the manufacturer to draw up an EU declaration of conformity
when placing a product with digital elements on the market, stating that the
essential cybersecurity requirements in Annex I have been fulfilled. Annex V
sets its contents. Article 28(3): where a product is subject to more than one
Union act requiring a declaration, a single declaration is drawn up covering all
of them. Article 13(20): a copy of the declaration, or a simplified declaration
under Annex VI carrying the internet address of the full one, must accompany the
product.

## Simplified declaration (Annex VI)

Article 13(20) allows a simplified declaration to accompany the product instead
of a full copy, provided it carries the exact internet address of the full one.
Annex VI fixes the wording:

```text
Hereby, [NAME OF MANUFACTURER] declares that the product with digital elements
type [DESIGNATION OF TYPE OF PRODUCT WITH DIGITAL ELEMENTS] is in compliance
with Regulation (EU) 2024/2847.

The full text of the EU declaration of conformity is available at the following
internet address: [URL]
```

## Notes for whoever eventually fills this in

**You cannot use this project's documents as your declaration.** The
declaration is a statement by a named legal person accepting responsibility.
What this repository provides is evidence you may cite in your technical
documentation — the SBOM, the risk assessment, the Annex I mapping — not a
conclusion you may adopt.

**Get the classification right before choosing a procedure.** The analysis in
[`README.md`](README.md) §5 concludes that superbackup is arguably an Annex III
Class I product (password managers, as described in Commission Implementing
Regulation (EU) 2025/2392) and that the project documents itself to that
standard. If your product is not itself free and open-source software with
public technical documentation, **Article 32(5) is not available to you**, and
with no harmonised standards published that means Module B+C or Module H, and a
notified body.

**CE marking follows the declaration, not the other way round.** Article 30
governs affixing it. Nothing may be CE-marked on the strength of this
repository.

**The support period is a commitment, not a field.** Article 13(8) requires at
least five years, or the expected use time if shorter, and Article 13(9)
requires each security update issued during that period to remain available for
at least 10 years after it was issued or for the remainder of the support
period, whichever is longer. Article 13(19) requires the end date to be visible
at the point of purchase. Decide it before you write it down.

**Keep the file.** Article 13(13): the technical documentation and the
declaration must be kept at the disposal of market surveillance authorities for
at least 10 years after the product is placed on the market, or for the support
period, whichever is longer.
